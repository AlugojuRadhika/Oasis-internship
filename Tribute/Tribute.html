Why I Admire Lord Shiva

Lord Shiva – The Supreme Yogi Lord Shiva, also known as Mahadeva (the Great God), is one of the principal deities in Hinduism, forming the Holy Trinity (Trimurti) alongside Brahma (the creator) and Vishnu (the preserver). Shiva is the destroyer and transformer, symbolizing the end of ignorance and the path to enlightenment.

The Divine Qualities of Lord Shiva

  • He resides on Mount Kailash in the Himalayas.
  • His vehicle is Nandi, the sacred bull.
  • The Shivalinga represents his formless and infinite nature.
